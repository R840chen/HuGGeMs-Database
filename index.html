HuGGeMs Database

A comprehensively curated resource designed for taxonomic profiling of human gut metagenomes. Integrating 228,837 high-quality genomes, 11,376 species-level genome bins (SGBs), and 1.86 million unique marker genes, compatible with the MetaPhlAn4 framework.
