About HuGGeMs

Overview

HuGGeMs (Human Gut Microbial Gene Markers) is a comprehensively curated resource designed for taxonomic profiling of human gut metagenomes, compatible with the MetaPhlAn4 framework. The database integrates 228,837 high-quality genomes (227,679 public + 1,158 newly isolated strains) into 11,376 species-level genome bins (SGBs), yielding 1.86 million unique marker genes.

Among the 1,158 newly sequenced isolates, 82 strains represent 41 previously undescribed or uncultured species, substantially expanding the diversity of human gut microbiota. Benchmarking using simulated metagenomes and synthetic communities demonstrated that HuGGeMs offers higher recall compared to existing mainstream reference databases, achieving a 48.6% increase in reads mapping rates over the default MetaPhlAn4 database across 7,646 real metagenomic samples.

Data Sources

The database aggregates genomes from six major repositories:

• NCBI GenBank   • UHGG v2.0.1   • hGMB   • HumGut   • GMrepo   • CGR v2

Genome sequences of cultured isolates: BioProject PRJNA1458945
